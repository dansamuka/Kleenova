Website privacy

Privacy information

This website is intentionally lightweight and does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

Quote form

The quote form is processed in your browser. When you submit it, the site creates a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp for you to review. The website itself does not store or transmit the form contents to a server.

Contacting Kleenova

If you choose to call, email or message Kleenova on WhatsApp, the information you provide is handled through the relevant communication service and by Kleenova for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and delivering services.

External services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Links to WhatsApp, email and telephone services are external to this website and those providers apply their own network and privacy terms. The Inter typeface is self-hosted with the website and does not require a separate font-network request.

Questions

For privacy questions connected to a cleaning enquiry, contact kleenova.cleaning@gmail.com.
