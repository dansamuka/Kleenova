Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before you book.

For anything specific to your property or workplace, send us a quick WhatsApp message.

Which areas do you serve?

Kleenova serves Nairobi and surrounding areas. Share your neighbourhood in the quote form and the team can confirm availability.

Can I book recurring cleaning?

Yes. Ask about a recurring schedule for residential, office or commercial cleaning and the team can confirm a frequency that fits your needs.

Do you use eco-friendly products?

Kleenova’s service positioning includes eco-friendly product use. For allergies, sensitive surfaces or special product requirements, mention them before the booking so the appropriate approach can be confirmed.

Can you clean healthcare spaces?

Healthcare cleaning is one of Kleenova’s service categories. Because requirements can vary significantly by facility, the scope and any site-specific protocols should be agreed before work begins.

How do I get a price?

Use the quote form or contact Kleenova directly. Pricing can then reflect the size, condition, service type, frequency and any special requirements instead of relying on a generic estimate.
