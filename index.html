Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect before you book.

If your space has specific access, surface, timing or product requirements, include them in your quote request.

Which areas do you serve?

Kleenova serves Nairobi and surrounding areas. Share your neighbourhood and the team will confirm availability for your location.

Can I book recurring cleaning?

Recurring cleaning can be discussed for residential, office and commercial spaces. Share the frequency you have in mind and the team can confirm availability.

Do you use eco-friendly products?

Eco-friendly products are part of Kleenova’s offering and are used where suitable. Mention allergies, sensitive surfaces or specific product requirements so suitability can be confirmed before the clean.

Can you clean healthcare spaces?

Healthcare cleaning is one of Kleenova’s service categories. Because requirements vary by facility, the scope, access and site-specific requirements are confirmed before work begins.

How is pricing worked out?

Kleenova quotes after the service type, scope, frequency and any special requirements are clear. Start with the basics, then the team confirms the price with you directly.
