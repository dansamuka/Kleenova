# Kleenova Brand Notes

## Source

Primary brand direction comes from the business artwork supplied for this project.

## Brand promise

**Clean Spaces. Healthier Lives.**

Supporting language:
- Professional
- Reliable
- Trusted
- Your space. Our priority.

## Core colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal | `#003F43` | Hero, footer, strong backgrounds |
| Primary teal | `#005D62` | Buttons, icons, headings |
| Accent teal | `#08757A` | Links, interactive accents |
| Soft mint | `#DFF3EF` | Icon/background tint |
| Gold | `#D6AD3F` | Premium accent, dividers, emphasis |
| Cream | `#FBFAF5` | Warm neutral backgrounds |
| Ink | `#0B2628` | Primary text |

## Art direction

- Bright, hygienic environments with natural daylight.
- Teal cleaning equipment may be used as a recognizable visual anchor.
- Gold is an accent, not the dominant color.
- Photography should look professional and believable rather than hyper-stylized.
- Avoid embedding generated text or logos into AI imagery; overlay real HTML/SVG branding instead.
- Prefer real Kleenova staff/location photography as the brand matures.

## Voice

Clear, reassuring and service-oriented. Avoid inflated claims. The site should sound professional without corporate jargon.
